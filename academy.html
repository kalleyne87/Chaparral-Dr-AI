The core idea

Same thing, new vocabulary

Most programming vocabulary is just a new name for something a student already understands.

Real world

Point at almost anything.

It's a thing. It has details, a color, a size, a name. It can do things: move, make a sound, turn on.

➜
In code

That thing becomes an object.

Its details are called attributes. What it can do is called an action, or a method. Same idea, new vocabulary.
