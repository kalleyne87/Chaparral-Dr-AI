How it works

Roots before branches

Automation fails when it is bolted on top. We learn the need as well as business context before proposing any solutions.

01 / Trace

We ask until it is clear

A working conversation with the people who do the task, not a questionnaire. We map every handoff, exception, and unwritten rule before proposing anything.

02 / Prototype

One workflow, working

A narrow build against your real data, running beside your team so you can compare its output to theirs before anyone commits.

03 / Wire in

Into your stack

Deployed in your cloud, using your identity provider and your data retention rules. We hand over the repo and the runbook.

04 / Tend

We keep it healthy

Models drift and processes change. We monitor accuracy, retune, and extend it to the next workflow when you are ready.
